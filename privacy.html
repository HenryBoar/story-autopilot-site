Privacy Policy — Reddit Story Autopilot

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Reddit Story Autopilot ("the app") is a personal, locally run tool that creates videos and uploads them to a YouTube channel owned by the person who runs it. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

When you authorize the app with your Google account, it receives permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload). It does not read your viewers' data, your analytics, your subscriptions, your messages, or any other Google data.

How data is used

The authorization is used only to upload videos that you create and approve. Video titles, descriptions, and tags are sent to YouTube as part of that upload.

What is stored, and where

  • Your OAuth token is stored only on your own computer, in secrets/youtube_token.json.
  • Videos, audio, captions, and settings are stored only on your own computer.
  • The app does not operate a server that receives your data, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

Third-party services

The app sends videos to YouTube using the YouTube Data API. Your use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The OAuth token remains on your computer until you delete it or revoke access. To revoke access, visit your Google Account permissions page and remove the app, then delete secrets/youtube_token.json. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
